About SIP Abacus

A proven method, taught the SIP way

SIP Abacus is a structured mental arithmetic program built around the centuries-old soroban abacus. Children first learn to calculate physically on the beads, then progress to visualising the abacus in their mind — solving multi-digit problems mentally, without a calculator.

Our curriculum is delivered in small batches by certified trainers across 8 progressive levels, with regular assessments so parents can track real, measurable growth in speed and accuracy.

Beyond arithmetic, the whole-brain engagement of abacus training is designed to sharpen focus, memory, and creative problem-solving — skills that carry over into every subject at school.
