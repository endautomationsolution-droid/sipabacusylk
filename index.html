About SIP Abacus Yelahanka New Town

At SIP Abacus Yelahanka New Town, we are committed to helping children unlock their true potential through fun, structured, and scientifically designed brain development programs.

Using the globally trusted SIP Abacus curriculum, children progress from physical abacus calculations to powerful mental arithmetic, building exceptional speed, accuracy, and confidence. Conducted in small batches by certified trainers, our classes provide personalised attention and regular assessments to ensure steady progress. Beyond mathematics, our program nurtures concentration, memory, logical thinking, creativity, and self-confidence — equipping children with essential skills for lifelong academic success.

Empowering Young Minds. Building Confident Futures.

Common questions from parents

What age can my child start? +
We accept students from age 5 onward. Our Junior track is designed specifically for first-time learners aged 5–7.
How long is each level? +
Most students complete a level in 3–4 months with weekly classes, though pace varies by child and practice frequency.
Do you offer online classes? +
Yes — every track is available online with a physical abacus kit shipped to your home, or in-centre at our Yelahanka branch.
Is the free demo class really free? +
Yes, with no obligation to enrol. It's a full-length class so your child can experience the teaching style firsthand.
How much practice is needed at home? +
We recommend 10–15 minutes of daily practice using the take-home worksheet pack included with every level.
